Privacy Policy

Souveno Social Scheduler (the "App"), operated by Souveno AI ("we", "us").
Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. About this App

The App is an internal tool used only by Souveno AI. It publishes Souveno AI's own marketing posts (images and captions) to Souveno AI's own social media accounts and pages: Facebook, Instagram, Threads, LinkedIn and X. It is not offered to the public and has no public sign-up.

2. Information we access

When the Souveno AI account owner connects the App, we receive access tokens that let the App publish to the connected accounts. We also receive the identifiers of those accounts (for example, a Page ID or user ID) so the App knows where to publish.

The App does not collect, read or store personal data of visitors, followers, customers or any other users of those platforms.

3. How we use information

Access tokens and account identifiers are used only to publish scheduled posts to Souveno AI's own accounts. We do not use them for advertising, profiling or analytics about other people, and we do not sell or share them.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored in the credential store of Souveno AI's own automation server and are not published or shared. Post content (images and captions) is the company's own marketing content. We take reasonable steps to protect this information.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share information with third parties, except that the App sends the posts we create to the social media platforms listed above, through their official APIs, in order to publish them.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep access tokens only while the App is in use. You can ask us to delete any data we hold, or the owner of a connected account can revoke the App's access at any time from that platform's settings (for example, Facebook Settings, Business Integrations, remove the App). To request deletion, email us at the address below and we will respond promptly.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted at this address with a new date.
