If you run a manufacturing business, you know one customer enquiry can mean many manual steps: understanding the requirement, checking stock, preparing the quotation, confirming the order and payment, raising the invoice and e-way bill, and arranging dispatch.

• Requirement collection
• Product and stock check (when connected)
• Quotation generation
• Order confirmation and payment follow-up
• Dispatch and e-way bill handling
• Automated invoice generation
• 24/7 customer engagement

Onboarding can be done in under 30 minutes.

Exact capabilities depend on the integrations and configuration for each business.
