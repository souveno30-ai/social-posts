In B2B manufacturing, a single customer enquiry usually kicks off a long chain of manual work: understand the requirement, check product and stock, prepare a quotation, follow up, confirm the order and payment, raise the invoice and e-way bill, and arrange dispatch. Every step handled by hand is a chance for delay or error.

E2D (Enquiry to Dispatch) by Souveno AI is built for manufacturing sales teams to automate this flow end to end.

E2D can support:

• Enquiries from customers on WhatsApp
• Requirement collection through conversation
• Product and stock check (when connected to your system)
• Quotation generation
• Order confirmation and payment follow-up
• Dispatch and e-way bill handling
• Automated invoice generation
• 24/7 customer engagement

Businesses can be onboarded in under 30 minutes. Your team focuses on the business, while E2D handles the routine flow.

The exact capabilities depend on the integrations and configuration for each business.
