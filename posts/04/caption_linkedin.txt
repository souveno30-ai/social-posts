In a warehouse, a lot happens at once: forklifts moving, people crossing aisles, loading bays busy and restricted areas that need strict control. Cameras are usually in place, but someone has to be watching at the right moment.

SOWI by Souveno AI adds an AI layer to compatible existing cameras so they can help keep the warehouse safer and smarter.

SOWI can support:

• Restricted-zone entry detection with real-time alerts
• People detection and movement visibility
• Vehicle movement monitoring
• Area monitoring across camera views
• Operational insights, such as people counts and working or not-working status
• Alerts to a phone with the event image

This helps safety and operations teams notice incidents earlier instead of reviewing footage after the fact.

The exact capability depends on camera quality, placement, lighting, environment and the configured use case.
