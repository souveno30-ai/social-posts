Warehouses are busy places: forklifts moving, people crossing aisles, loading bays active and restricted areas that need strict control. Most already have cameras, but cameras alone only record.

SOWI by Souveno AI adds an AI layer to compatible existing cameras to help make the warehouse safer and smarter.

SOWI can support:

• Restricted-zone entry detection with real-time alerts
• People detection and movement visibility
• Vehicle movement monitoring
• Area monitoring across camera views
• Operational insights, such as people counts and working or not-working status
• Alerts to your phone with the event image

The exact capability depends on camera quality, placement, lighting, environment and the configured use case.
