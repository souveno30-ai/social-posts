Factories already have cameras across production areas, entrances and work zones. SOWI adds an AI layer to compatible CCTV systems so those camera feeds can support more than just recording.

SOWI can support:

• Configured PPE-related safety events
• Restricted-zone monitoring
• Person and movement detection
• Employee presence and activity visibility
• Event records and alerts
• Camera-based operational insights
• Compatible CCTV, IP camera, NVR and RTSP environments

Instead of using camera footage only after something happens, SOWI helps businesses make better use of the camera infrastructure they already have for day-to-day safety and operations.
