Factories already have CCTV cameras covering production areas, entrances and critical work zones. In many businesses, those cameras are mainly used for recording and reviewing footage later.

SOWI adds an AI layer to compatible existing camera infrastructure so businesses can use those same feeds for selected safety and operational use cases.

SOWI can support:

• Configured PPE-related safety events
• Restricted and high-risk zone monitoring
• Person detection and movement visibility
• Employee presence and activity visibility
• Event records and alerts for important activity
• Camera-based operational insights
• Compatible CCTV, IP camera, NVR and RTSP environments

This helps businesses move from simply recording footage toward getting more useful operational visibility from the camera systems already in place.

The exact capability depends on the camera quality, placement, lighting, environment and the configured AI use case.
