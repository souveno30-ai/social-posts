Factories already have cameras across production areas and work zones. SOWI adds an AI layer to compatible CCTV systems so those feeds can support configured PPE events, restricted-zone monitoring, person detection, movement visibility and operational alerts.

The aim is simple: make existing cameras more useful for day-to-day safety and operations.
