Turn service reminders into booked visits. 🚗

A service reminder only helps if it ends in a booking. SouvAAK can call customers, capture their preferred time and send the request to your service team.

Capabilities include:

• Calls customers due for service
• Asks date, time and vehicle details
• Sends the booking request to your service team

A typical call: "Hello! Your vehicle is due for service. Would you like to book a slot this week?" The customer can book, reschedule or raise a query.

Exact capabilities depend on the integrations and configuration set up for each business.
