Every hospital front desk knows this problem: the phone keeps ringing while patients are waiting at the counter. Calls about OPD timings, doctor availability, appointments, lab reports and prescription refills take up a large part of the day, and missed calls mean missed appointments.

SOUVAAK is a voice AI for hospitals that helps handle these calls automatically, around the clock.

SOUVAAK can support:

• 24/7 call handling, so patient calls are not missed
• Appointment booking by department and date
• Patient information such as OPD timings and departments
• Enquiry and follow-up tracking
• A dashboard with calls, bookings and follow-ups
• Support for 9+ Indian languages, including Telugu and Hindi

The result is a lighter workload for reception and support teams, and a smoother experience for patients and families.

The exact capabilities depend on the integrations and configuration set up for each hospital.
